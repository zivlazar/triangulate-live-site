1. What Triangulate is

Triangulate is a team-based outdoor map game. Each team has three player slots. A slot can be filled by an outdoor real player or an AI player.

Outdoor players move using live device location. AI players are computer-controlled players provided by Triangulate. Solo play is a separate single-player practice scenario.

The main way to start a game is play a game. The lobby lets players build an outdoor team, invite friends, find teammates, add AI, and choose an enemy type. Enemy: Local | Country | Global | AI may be available as the enemy choice.
