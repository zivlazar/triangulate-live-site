Privacy policy

1. What Triangulate is

Triangulate is a team-based map game. Each team has three player slots. A player can take part as At home, Outside, or AI. At home players move by tapping the map. Outside players move using live device location. AI players are computer-controlled. The personal data we collect depends on how you choose to play.

2. Children and family play

Triangulate is intended for a general audience. Children under 13 should use Triangulate only with explicit parent or guardian permission and supervision. Parents or guardians are responsible for deciding whether a child may play Outside.

Outside play uses live location and may involve real-world movement. Triangulate does not knowingly collect child-specific personal data, does not create child-specific profiles, does not sell personal data, and does not use precise location for targeted advertising.

3. Information we collect

  • Account and profile information: a randomly generated player ID, nickname, colour, recovery code, preferences, and consent records.
  • Lobby and team information: team slots, role choice, invite code use, team colour, team name, ready state, blockers, and enemy choice.
  • Location and movement information: At home tap routes, selected play area, Outside live device location, location accuracy, timestamps, speed, direction, route data, and fair-play signals where needed.
  • Gameplay information: match participation, round results, wins, duration, arena type, AI usage, enemy type, and unranked mixed-play results.
  • Device information: device type, platform, app state, diagnostics, and network quality signals used for compatibility and reliability.
  • Analytics events: app and website events used to improve gameplay, fix bugs, test fairness, and monitor safety. We do not use your phone number as an automatic analytics identifier.

4. Location and movement information

Your role choice controls location use. In compliance with PECR Regulation 14, we only process location data with your explicit consent for the value-added service of playing Triangulate Outside.

At home: uses tap-to-move gameplay, does not require live GPS for movement. If you choose Use my area, device location may be used to set the arena.

Outside: uses live device location, requires usable GPS, may require Precise Location depending on your device, uses fair-play and safety checks. You can turn off location access in your device settings at any time.

5. Who can see your location or movement

During a match, other players in the same match (teammates and enemy players) may see your player marker on the game map. This can include your teammates and enemy players.

If you play Outside, your player marker is based on your live device location while Outside play is active. If you play At home, your player marker is based on your tap-to-move route, not live GPS.

6. How we use location to create an arena

A match takes place in one arena. If one or more friendly players choose Outside, the arena is based on the real-world area of the Outside player or players. If multiple friendly players choose Outside, we check if they are close enough to share one real-world arena.

7. AI players

AI players are computer-controlled. They do not represent a real person, and they do not have personal location data. AI movement is generated by the game using simulated road or path behaviour.

8. Mixed play and rankings

Mixed play is unranked in this version. Mixed-play games do not submit leaderboard scores, public wins, public losses, streaks, or public team statistics. Internal analytics may still be used to test fairness, improve gameplay, fix bugs, and monitor safety.

9. How we use your information

We use information to provide the game, build teams, sync lobbies, move player markers, create arenas, match enemies, provide AI fallback, show game state, run unranked mixed play, improve gameplay, fix bugs, monitor safety, prevent cheating, and operate events and support.

10. Third-party services

We use services for hosting, maps, analytics, app distribution, email, social media, and operational support. These services are contractually bound to protect your data and are not used to sell your precise location to advertisers.

  • Supabase: backend database and server functions.
  • Mixpanel: analytics for gameplay and product events.
  • MapLibre / Protomaps: vector maps loaded on demand.

11. Data retention

Gameplay and profile data may be retained while your profile is active. Consent records are retained to document which legal version was accepted. We retain location, movement, and analytics data for a maximum of 12 months for operational analysis and improvement, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

12. Your rights (UK GDPR)

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, correction, erasure, restriction of processing, objection to processing, and data portability. You can change app permissions in device settings. To exercise any of these rights, please use our contact form.

13. Website-specific notes

This website is a marketing site for Triangulate. If you browse the site without contacting us, we only use analytics when you explicitly consent. If you use the contact form, we store the details you submit so we can respond and keep a record of feedback.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the Last updated date at the top of this page or publish a new legal version.
